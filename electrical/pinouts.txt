ARDUINO PINOUT
---------------

NAME               | PIN
-------------------+------
CAN_TX             | D10
CAN_RX             | D9
ELEVATION_MOTOR_P  | A0
ELEVATION_MOTOR_M  | A1
ELEVATION_ENC_A    | D5
ELEVATION_ENC_B    | D6
ELEVATION_HALL     | D7
AZIMUTH_MOTOR_P    | A2
AZIMUTH_MOTOR_M    | A3
AZIMUTH_ENC_A      | D2
AZIMUTH_ENC_B      | D3
AZIMUTH_HALL       | D4

Firmware pin *roles* (including encoder A/B sense) live in config/system.yaml
under `pins:` and are baked into firmware via `uv run hotbox-gen-firmware-geometry`.
Swap elevation_enc_a/b or azimuth_enc_a/b there to invert quadrature direction.
The table above is the board harness silk → Arduino pad map.
