MOTOR CONNECTOR
---------------

NAME         | COLOR  | CIRCULAR_PIN  | JST_PIN 
-------------+--------+---------------+---------
MOTOR+       | RED    | 1             | 2-1  
MOTOR-       | BLACK  | 2             | 2-2
ENC/HALL_GND | GREY   | 7             | 6-1
ENC_VCC      | BLUE   | 6             | 6-2
ENC_A        | YELLOW | 3             | 6-4
ENC_B        | WHITE  | 4             | 6-5
HALL_VCC     | BROWN  | 8             | 6-3
HALL_OUT     | GREEN  | 5             | 6-6


INTERFACE CONNECTOR
-------------------

NAME         | COLOR  | JST_PIN
-------------+--------+----------
GND          | BLACK  | 1
VCC_12V      | RED    | 2
CAN_H        | YELLOW | 3
CAN_L        | GREEN  | 4

Firmware pin *roles* (including encoder A/B sense) live in config/system.yaml
under `pins:` and are baked into firmware via `uv run hotbox-gen-firmware-geometry`.
Swap elevation_enc_a/b or azimuth_enc_a/b there to invert quadrature direction.
